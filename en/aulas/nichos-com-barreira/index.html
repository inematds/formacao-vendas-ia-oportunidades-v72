Lesson 7 · 15 min

Niches with a barrier: opportunities 30 to 36

All seven in this group are building: dormant-base reactivation, finance, events, health, public tenders, visualization, and data extraction. What they have in common is the barrier, and the barrier is trust: a contact who gave permission, a notice said in big letters, a decision that is not yours, and a guarantee only with a measured baseline.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1Why all seven are building
  2. 2The trust piece each one requires
  3. 3Sell research and tools, never the decision
  4. 4Measure the baseline before you promise

Goal: check contact authorization, notices, limits, and baseline before proposing anything in a niche with a barrier.

01 · Opportunities 30 to 36 in the filter

All of them are building. Three of them carry a paper trust piece. Click each box.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

A barrier is not a reason to run from the niche. It's what separates the people who have confidence from the people who only have a tool.

02 · The four barriers

One question for each, before any proposal.

1. Permission

Did the old contacts agree to receive messages? Without that there is no proposal.

And before you quote a percentage, understand their math: gross margin is not net margin, and there is a cost to serve each sale.

2. The decision isn't yours

Sell tools and research, never investment recommendations. Whoever sells research so others can decide has a business; whoever sells the decision has exposure.

This is not legal or financial advice: it's the line between product and risk.

3. Warnings

If you are not a health professional, that has to be stated everywhere, in large type: each person follows up with their own doctor, and nothing there is clinical guidance.

4. Baseline

Measure how many closings per week there were before, track four weeks without changing anything else, and state the whole comparison.

Promise an absolute number, not a percentage: a percentage is easy to argue about later.

03 · Template to copy

Fill this in before you propose in a niche with a barrier.

Barrier checklist before proposing
CHECKLIST DE BARREIRA · <empresa> · <data>

[ ] DADOS: os contatos/dados que vou usar foram autorizados para esse uso? Quem confirmou, por escrito?
[ ] LIMITE: estou vendendo ferramenta/pesquisa ou estou decidindo pelo cliente? (decidir = fora)
[ ] AVISOS: preciso de aviso de que não sou profissional da área? Onde ele aparece?
[ ] MATEMÁTICA: sei a margem líquida e o custo de atender cada venda antes de dizer um número?
[ ] LINHA DE BASE: qual é o número de antes, em que período, e o que não pode mudar durante a medição?
[ ] ESPECIALISTA: em que ponto eu chamo alguém habilitado (jurídico, saúde, finanças)?

Se algum item ficou sem resposta, a proposta espera.

04 · Practice

Two exercises.

1 · Checklist on a real business

25 min

Pick a business you know that has a contact list that has been idle for more than six months (store, clinic, school). Fill in the checklist with what you know and mark what you need to ask.

Done when: all 6 items have an answer or the exact question you will ask, and the DADOS item says who confirms the permission.

2 · A defined baseline

15 min

For that business, write which number you would measure before any promise, and enter 30 through 36 in the filter table.

Done when: you have the metric, the "before" period, the length of the tracking, and what cannot change midway; the 7 rows are in the table.

05 · Quick test

3 questions.

Question 1 of 3

You are going to propose commission-based list reactivation. What do you need to know before you quote the percentage?

Question 2 of 3

In the financial market, what can you safely sell?

Question 3 of 3

When can you offer a results guarantee on a visualization tool?
