Lesson 2 · 18 min

The trust funnel and the 15-minute conversation

Nobody buys on the first sentence. Between "this person exists" and "this person is going to get my money" there are small steps, and each one is a separate sale. In the 43 Opportunities the path is the same: proof, lead magnet, conversation, diagnostic and follow-up. The piece that decides everything is the 15-minute conversation.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1The five funnel steps that appear in all 43
  2. 2Why a good lead magnet is finished work
  3. 3The script for the 15-minute conversation
  4. 4Diagnostic and roadmap: two different services

Goal: run the 15-minute diagnostic conversation and propose the next paid step without selling building before its time.

01 · The trust funnel

Five steps. Only the last two involve payment. Click each one.

Click each block in the diagram to see what it does.

The first three steps bill nothing, and they are what make the last two exist.

02 · The 15-minute script

Five moves. The time for each is in parentheses.

1. Frame

State the rule of the conversation right at the start. It takes the pressure off both sides.

LineWe have 15 minutes to see if it makes sense to work together. If it doesn't, I'll tell you right then.

2. Narrow it down

The classic mistake is opening with "how do you think about using AI?" and getting five minutes of venting, much of it complaints. The tool is the one-sentence constraint.

LineIn just one sentence: what is the problem we're trying to solve?

3. Quantify

How many times a week it happens, how much time it takes, how much revenue doesn't come in. Each question is answered with a number, not an opinion.

4. Propose and close

Propose the paid diagnostic with the deadline and price said out loud, no beating around the bush. Finishing before the agreed time signals more competence than stretching it to an hour.

The stance is calm indifference: you lead like someone who has other commitments. It's not arrogance, it's structure.

    04 · And the price?

    Pricing has an owner in the program. The pricing ladder and the menu of options are in course 2, Consulting Playbook. The price ranges by service and "price is a test" are in course 4, From Zero to First Client.

    From here, take one rule: the price comes from the value the client receives, not from the hours you spend. And the short diagnostic today works better as a lead magnet, because the client can get something similar on their own with an AI tool. What still holds is judgment: what to prioritize, what the AI gets wrong, and the right order.

    05 · Template to copy

    Run it before your next real conversation.

    Prompt · your 15-minute script
    Build the script for a 15-minute diagnostic conversation.
I offer <the role you chose: consultant, auditor, mentor or trainer> to <type of company>.

Deliver, in this order:
1. The opening line that frames the conversation in 30 seconds.
2. The one-sentence restriction question, written the way I would say it out loud.
3. Five quantification questions that turn his pain into a number per month.
   Each one has to be answerable with a number, not with an opinion.
4. The line that proposes the paid next step (diagnostic or roadmap), with a deadline and a price stated without apology.
5. Three signs, during the conversation, that this person will NOT buy, so I can end early.
