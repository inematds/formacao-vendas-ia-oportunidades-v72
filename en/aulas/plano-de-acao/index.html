  1. 1/5

    Choose and prove

    Run the candidates from the table through the five criteria and keep one. Run it once, for free, for someone you know: you need your own proof before any conversation.

  2. 2/5

    Lead magnet and list

    Turn what you did into a repeatable lead magnet. Build the list of twenty companies and write the five-line message, with no link.

  3. 3/5

    Batch and conversation

    Send to all twenty. Note how many replied and how many booked a conversation. Don't change anything in the middle of the batch: without a closed batch there is no information, only feeling.

  4. 4/5

    Propose and adjust

    Run the conversations with the script from lesson 2. Change one variable for the next batch and make at least one proposal, with no apology.

  5. 5/5

    The whole cycle

    Filter, lead magnet, conversation and proposal. Lessons 4 to 7 go into the plan generated below, with the full table of the 43.

A batch is closed: change only one variable between one batch and the next. Only after three batches can you say "it doesn't work".

03 · Template to copy

The written plan with goals in numbers.

Prompt · your 30-day plan
You are my project manager and you are tough on me. I am going to run ONE service over the next 4 weeks.
Chosen role: <consultant, auditor, mentor or trainer>
Service: <what I deliver, e.g.: short diagnostic + roadmap>
My context: I have <hours per week> free, <available cash>, and I know how to do <skills>.
Target niche: <sector and focus, e.g.: dental clinics with 2 offices in my city>

Build:
1. The tasks for each week, at most 3 per week, each one fitting my real time.
2. The numeric goals for each week (lead magnets produced, contacts, conversations).
3. What I must have DONE on day 30 to say it was worth it, in facts, not in feeling.
4. The 3 signs that I chose wrong and should switch on day 30, each one checkable with a number.
5. The 5 most likely excuses I will use to avoid doing it, and the answer to each one.
No "stay committed". I want tasks with a verb, a number and a day.
